Alza Price Insight – price history, smart watching and the real unit price for Alza

Do you shop on alza.cz, alza.sk or alza.hu? Alza Price Insight tells you whether today's price is really a good deal – right in your shopping lists, on product pages and in category listings.

PRICE HISTORY
• Every price of a product in one place: regular, sale, AlzaPlus+, discount code, cashback, trade-in, benefit/B2B and Black Friday
• Chart of all price types with availability, all-time low, highest selling price and lowest price in the last 30 days
• Table of every price and availability change
• Works in shopping lists and on product pages

IS THE PRICE REALLY GOOD?
• Usual price – what the product sold for most of the last 180 days, so a short sale doesn't distort it
• Clear rating: lowest in X days / X % below usual / at the usual price / X % above usual – shown as ▼ and ▲ arrows in your lists
• Suspicious discounts: a warning when the product was cheaper in the last 30 days than its current "sale" price
• Made-up strikethrough prices: a warning when the product never actually sold at the crossed-out price

TARGET PRICE
• Set the price you want to pay and get a notification as soon as the price drops to it – even with Alza closed
• Target per piece for multipacks, e.g. 2.40 Kč per piece

BACKGROUND WATCHING
• Watch whole shopping lists or individual products – checked every hour, or just once a day at a time you choose
• Notifications for price drops (with an optional minimum %), new prices (sale, code, cashback) and availability changes
• Quiet hours, number of changes on the extension icon, overview of changes in the popup

REAL PRICE PER PIECE
• Alza calculates the unit price without AlzaPlus+ and discount codes. The extension shows the real price per piece in categories, search results, shopping lists and on product pages
• The pack size selector shows the price per piece for every size and highlights the best value
• Sort a whole category by the real unit price – ideal for stocking up during sales

SMARTER SHOPPING LISTS
• Summary bar: what you pay including volume discounts, cashback shown separately, savings, items that got cheaper or more expensive since last time
• Filters: cheaper since last time, on sale, below usual price, worth waiting, volume discount, cheaper in another condition, in stock, target reached, suspicious discount
• Sale rhythm: how often the product goes on sale and when the next sale is due – "worth waiting"
• The same product cheaper unpacked or like new
• Sorting: cheapest, cheapest per piece, most expensive, biggest discount, furthest below usual price, biggest drop

YOUR DATA UNDER YOUR CONTROL
• Backup and restore (JSON), price history export to CSV for Excel
• Suggestions to clean up old products you no longer follow
• A diagnostics file you can send to the author if something goes wrong

LANGUAGES
Czech, Slovak, Hungarian and English. You can change the extension's language in the settings – otherwise it follows your browser and the language of the Alza site.

HOW TO START
1. Log in to Alza and keep prices shown including VAT. Prices differ per account, so they are saved only when you are logged in.
2. Open one of your shopping lists – the extension loads all prices and starts recording their history.
3. Turn on watching in the extension popup, or set a target price on a product page.
The built-in help (question mark in the popup) explains everything step by step.

The price rating and the strikethrough-price check need at least 14 days of recorded history for a product.

PRIVACY
All data (prices, history, settings) is stored only locally in your browser. The extension communicates only with Alza websites and sends nothing anywhere else. No account, no tracking, no ads.

PERMISSIONS
• Access to alza.cz, alza.sk and alza.hu – to show prices on Alza pages and load product prices
• Storage – to keep price history and settings in your browser
• Alarms – to check watched products regularly in the background (every hour up to once a day)
• Notifications – to tell you about price changes and reached target prices
